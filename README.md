# Solar & Battery Financials

[![Open your Home Assistant instance and open a repository inside the Home Assistant Community Store.](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/hacs_repository.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/hacs_repository/?owner=Zuanism&repository=solar-battery-financials&category=integration)

A custom integration for Home Assistant that provides deep, real-time financial tracking of your Solar PV, Home Battery storage system, and individual household appliances. 

Unlike standard energy dashboards that only show raw kWh, this integration acts as a live financial simulator. It continuously answers fundamental questions about your home energy economics:
1. **How much money is my entire system saving me right now?**
2. **How much money would I be saving if I only had solar panels (no battery)?**
3. **What is the exact financial value my battery is adding (or costing) me at this exact moment?**
4. **Exactly how much is each individual appliance costing me to run, taking into account my live dynamic tariffs, solar generation, and battery state?**

By tracking instantaneous power flows and live electricity prices, it accurately splits your savings between "Solar Earnings" and "Battery Added Value," fully accounting for real-world inefficiencies like DC-to-AC conversion losses and feed-in penalties. Furthermore, it dynamically applies an "Opportunity Cost" model to track the true cost of your individual devices.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Real-Time Financial Math:** Calculates live `€/h` (or your local currency) savings based on your dynamic electricity price sensor.
- **"No-Battery" Simulator:** Runs a parallel simulation in the background to calculate what your grid import/export *would have been* if your battery didn't exist. 
- **Dynamic Inverter Efficiency Tracking:** (If an Inverter AC sensor is provided) The integration dynamically calculates the real-time conversion efficiency of your inverter. When your battery charges, it applies this live efficiency curve to the simulation to accurately model the DC-to-AC losses you *would* have experienced.
- **Appliance-Level Tracking:** Track individual devices (e.g. Airco, Kitchen, Dishwasher) to see exactly how much they cost to run.
- **Sub-Devices & Hierarchies:** Track sub-devices (like a Dishwasher inside the Kitchen) without double-counting their power usage against your total house load.
- **Untracked Power Detection:** Automatically calculates a live "Untracked" power and cost sensor, showing you exactly how much "mystery power" your house is consuming outside of your known tracked devices.
- **Native Energy Dashboard Support:** Generates Riemann Sum Integral sensors (`kWh` and `€`) that are perfectly formatted with `state_class: total` to be used directly in Home Assistant Energy Dashboards and Utility Meters.
- **Hot-Reload Options:** Change your configuration (like price penalties, tracked devices, or sub-devices) directly from the UI without restarting Home Assistant.

## ⚙️ Configuration

You can set up this integration entirely via the Home Assistant UI (Config Flow). Go to **Settings -> Devices & Services -> Add Integration -> Solar & Battery Financials**.

### Required Sensors
You must provide the following standard power sensors (in Watts or kW):
* **Grid Sensor**: Instantaneous power to/from the grid. *(Positive = Importing from grid, Negative = Exporting to grid)*
* **Electricity Price Sensor**: A sensor providing your current electricity price per kWh (e.g., from Nordpool, Amber Electric, or a static template sensor).

### Optional Settings
* **Solar PV Sensor**: Raw DC power generated by the solar panels. *(Positive = Generating)*. If omitted, the integration will simulate a grid-only setup.
* **Battery Sensor**: Power flowing in/out of the battery. *(Positive = Discharging to house, Negative = Charging from PV/Grid)*. If omitted, the integration will simulate a setup without energy storage.
* **Inverter AC Sensor**: The AC output power of your hybrid inverter. If provided, the integration will use this to track exact hardware conversion losses. If left blank, the integration falls back to a lossless assumption (`AC Output = PV + Battery`) and uses a static 96% efficiency for simulations.
* **Fixed Feed-in Penalty**: A fixed deduction applied to your exported power price (e.g. `0.02 €/kWh`). Default is `0.0`.
* **Percentage Feed-in Penalty**: A percentage reduction applied to the spot price for exports (e.g., enter `20` for a 20% penalty where provider pays 80% of spot price). Default is `0.0`.
  * *Formula:* `Export Price = Spot Price × (1 - Percentage/100) - Fixed Fee`
  * *Tip:* Entering a **Negative** value (e.g., `-10%` or `-0.05 €`) mathematically acts as a **Feed-in Bonus / Subsidy**!
* **Entity Prefix**: A string prepended to all generated entities (Default: `sbf_`).
* **Tracked Devices**: A list of independent power sensors (e.g., smart plugs) to track for financial cost.
* **Sub-Devices**: A subset of your tracked devices that represent parts of larger groups. Their power is calculated for cost independently, but excluded from the "Untracked" math to avoid double counting.

## 📊 Generated Sensors

The integration generates a suite of sensors. For each metric, it creates a live **Rate Sensor** (e.g., `€/h`) and a cumulative **Integral Sensor** (e.g., `€`).

### 1. Power & Cost Baselines
* **Total Power Consumption**: Your true house load. Calculated seamlessly as `Grid + Inverter AC` (using the raw sensor values).
* **Total Cost Rate**: What you *would* be paying right now if you had no solar and no battery (House Load × Price).
* **Net Grid Cost Rate**: What you are *actually* paying (or earning) from the grid right now.

### 2. System Earnings
* **System Earnings Rate (`system_earnings_rate`)**: The total financial savings of your entire setup. 
  * *Math:* `Total Cost Rate - Net Grid Cost Rate`
* **System Earnings (`system_earnings`)**: The cumulative total money saved.

### 3. Solar Only Earnings (The Simulator)
* **Solar Only Earnings Rate (`solar_only_earnings_rate`)**: What your system *would* be saving you if you had the solar panels, but the battery was entirely removed.
  * *Math:* Calculates a simulated Grid Import/Export by subtracting raw PV generation (adjusted for dynamic inverter efficiency) from your house load.
* **Solar Earnings (`solar_earnings`)**: Cumulative money saved purely by the panels.

### 4. Battery Added Value
* **Battery Added Value Rate (`battery_added_value_rate`)**: The isolated financial impact of your battery. 
  * *Math:* `System Earnings Rate - Solar Only Earnings Rate`
  * *Note:* This sensor will routinely go **Negative** during the day when the battery is charging. This is mathematically correct! By charging the battery, you are forfeiting the immediate cash you could have made by exporting that solar power to the grid. It goes highly **Positive** at night when it discharges to save you from buying peak-rate grid power, allowing you to track the true round-trip ROI of the battery.
* **Battery Added Value (`battery_added_value`)**: The cumulative financial ROI of the battery hardware.

### 5. Individual Appliance Costs & Energy
For every device added to the "Tracked Devices" configuration, the integration dynamically creates:
* **[Device] Power (`_power`)**: The raw power draw of the device.
* **[Device] Energy Rate (`_energy_rate`)**: Live kW consumption rate.
* **[Device] Cost Rate (`_cost_rate`)**: Live €/h cost rate based on the Opportunity Cost model.
* **[Device] Cumulative Cost & Energy**: Cumulative € and kWh used.

### 6. The "Untracked" Sensors
* **Untracked Power & Cost Rate (`untracked_power` / `untracked_cost_rate`)**: Automatically subtracts the sum of all your "Tracked Devices" (ignoring those marked as "Sub-Devices") from your Total House Load. This tells you exactly how much power and money your unmonitored "dumb" appliances are costing you in real-time.

## 🧠 How the Math Works (Advanced)

### Tracked Device Pricing (Opportunity Cost)
When you track independent devices, the integration calculates their cost using a **Real-Time Opportunity Cost** philosophy. The `Effective Price` applied to your devices dynamically shifts based on what is happening at your utility meter at that exact second:

1. **Exporting to the Grid:** When your solar production exceeds your house load and battery charging, the effective price is the `Export Price` (Dynamic Tariff - Penalty). Running a device simply means you export less, so the cost is the foregone revenue.
2. **Running on Battery (Self-Sufficient):** When running purely off the battery (Grid = 0 W), the effective price remains the `Export Price`. We do not charge the full dynamic tariff here to prevent double-counting your Total House Costs later when the battery actually runs out and imports.
3. **Importing from the Grid (Blended Average):** When your house requires more power than solar/battery can provide, you pull from the grid. The effective price becomes a `Blended Average` between the Export Price and the full Dynamic Import Price. For example, if your house is using 2 kW, but 1 kW is coming from Solar and 1 kW is coming from the Grid, the effective price is set exactly halfway between the two. This ensures that the sum cost of all your tracked devices always perfectly matches your Total House Gross Bill!

### Inverter Efficiency
If you provide an `Inverter AC Sensor`, the integration calculates live hardware efficiency:
```python
DC_Power_to_Inverter = max(Raw_Solar, 0) + Raw_Battery
Instant_Efficiency = Inverter_AC_Output / DC_Power_to_Inverter
```
This ensures that when the battery is charging (and siphoning DC power away from the AC stage), the simulation accurately applies real-world conversion losses to the theoretical "what if I had no battery" solar export.

### The "Negative" Battery Value
When the sun is shining and the battery is charging:
- **System Earnings** captures the savings of powering your house, but misses out on export revenue because the PV power is going into the battery.
- **Solar Only Earnings** assumes the battery doesn't exist, so it assumes all excess PV is being exported to the grid for cash.
- Therefore, **Battery Added Value** becomes negative, representing the "opportunity cost" of storing the energy. 

Over a 24-hour cycle, the `Battery Added Value` integral sensor will track the net difference. If you charge during cheap solar/off-peak and discharge during expensive evening peaks, the integral will rise, perfectly tracking the battery's payoff over time!

## 🎨 Instant Dashboard Setup (Native Lovelace Strategy)

The integration bundles a native **Lovelace Dashboard Strategy**. You can generate a stunning, glassmorphic multi-tab energy dashboard in seconds without manual YAML coding!

### Prerequisites (HACS UI Cards)
Since the strategy dynamically renders cards in your browser, you must install these three custom UI cards via HACS Frontend:
- `apexcharts-card`
- `mushroom`
- `power-flow-card-plus`

### Dashboard Creation
Create a new blank dashboard in Storage mode (`mode: storage`), click the **3 dots -> Edit Dashboard -> Raw Configuration Editor**, and paste:

```yaml
strategy:
  type: custom:solar-battery-financials
```

This auto-generates:
1. **Power Landing Tab:** Real-time animated flow card + live wattage cards for all tracked & subset devices.
2. **Financials Tab:** Global period chips (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Yearly`, `Cumulative`) + live financial grids.
3. **Drill-Down Subviews:** Clicking any appliance or card natively transitions to a detailed historical breakdown graph!

*(Note: In storage dashboards, you can click **Take Control of Dashboard** at any time to convert the live strategy into standard editable UI cards!)*
